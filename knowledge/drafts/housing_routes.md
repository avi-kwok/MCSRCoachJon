# Housing bastion: route decision table

Every claim cites [n]. Learner edits win. Times are 2021 practice-map timings (trade start / hole return), not ranked [1].

## Decision variables (visible to the player)
1. **Arrival height: top or bottom.** Metacor: "if Bot: Manhunt"; "if Top: Top Down Manhunt" [2].
2. **Lava in the courtyard (flowing lava).** pncakespoon's sheet marks Manhunt / Top-Down as "Lava Flowing: No" and Lava Manhunt / Elevator as "Lava Flowing: Yes" [1].
3. **Left rampart: triple / single / ruins.** Top-Down Double Triple needs left rampart = Triple Chest [1]. Lava Top-Down has a separate "Ruin configuration route" [3].
4. **Right rampart.** Learner: always triple. > UNVERIFIED / CONFLICT: MCSR Wiki has a video "Lava Housing Ruined Right Rampart" ("very rare usecase") [4]. Learner to check whether a ruined right rampart is possible.
5. **Gold armour on entry.** Manhunt punch step is pointless if you entered wearing gold armour (bastions.md [6]). But MCSR Wiki (2026): "Always punch a piglin even after breaking a chest" because a piglin that first saw you without gold armour can stay in tier-1 aggro [5]. > CONFLICT: learner to pick which rule the coach uses.
6. Centre-chest location / direction to the ramparts (Manhunt start) [2].
