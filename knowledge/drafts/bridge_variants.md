## Names
- **TTC = Triple To Chalice**: loot a triple rampart first, then go to the chalice [4][5][6]. The opposite is the "Chalice to Triple" routes (DK/CS/soog in bridge_routes.md) [5].
- **LTTC / RTTC**: left/right triple to chalice [5][7][8].

## When a rampart start beats walking to the chalice
- kindred: TTC is "still much better than doing Chalice to Triple routes" [5]. But "if you are under the Diamond rank … dont expect to save time with it" (Lodesteal) [5].
- Bank Heist: time-neutral vs running to the chalice **with TNT**, faster **without TNT** [7].
- So the inputs are: which triple is known, whether you are already at that rampart (approach side), TNT in inventory, and the player's rank/comfort with the route.
> UNVERIFIED: no source gives a rule like "approaching from the left side → always LTTC". Learner to confirm the decision rule.
