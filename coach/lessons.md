# Approved coach lessons

General rules the learner has approved, learned from critic passes. Included in the critic pack (and,
once integrated, in coach-pack). Each lesson is a GENERAL rule — never a run-specific answer
(no timestamps, coordinates or "in run X the route was Y").

Workflow: the critic proposes lessons → `python -m vodreview add-lessons <file> --run <id>` appends them to
`coach/pending_lessons.md` → the learner moves the ones they approve here (edit wording freely) and
deletes the rest.

Format: `- **L<n>** (<rubric item>) <rule>` — optionally followed by ` _(from <run>, <date>)_`.
